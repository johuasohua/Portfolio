Rooter × OR Esports

Rooter Exclusive

How an exclusive 24-month platform partnership transformed Rooter into India's premier esports streaming destination, delivering 50M+ views and establishing the blueprint for future platform collaborations.

The Challenge

Rooter was entering one of India's most competitive gaming categories, taking on established platforms such as YouTube Gaming and Loco. While the platform had the technology, it lacked the exclusive content and creator ecosystem needed to attract gaming audiences and establish itself as a credible destination for live esports.

Competing on technology alone wasn't viable. Rooter needed a strategic content anchor: one that would signal to creators and audiences that this was a platform serious about esports.

The Idea

I brokered a 24-month exclusive partnership between Rooter and OR Esports, turning one of India's leading esports organizations into Rooter's flagship content partner.

The strategy was built around audience migration rather than audience acquisition. Instead of asking viewers to switch platforms immediately, creators would begin their livestreams on YouTube before directing their audiences to continue watching exclusively on Rooter. This reduced friction while steadily introducing millions of existing fans to the platform.

To accelerate adoption, Rooter also secured the exclusive digital broadcast rights to Legends Rise, OR Esports' flagship BGMI tournament, giving the platform premium esports content from day one.

"Exclusive content doesn't build platforms; exclusive audiences do. By controlling where fans watch, you control where creators stream."

The Execution

The partnership brought together 16 professional esports athletes and gaming creators, who streamed exclusively on Rooter over the 24-month period. The content strategy combined:

  • Competitive scrims and ranked gameplay from pro players
  • Creator-led entertainment streams and community content
  • Two annual editions of Legends Rise tournament, streamed exclusively
  • Consistent pipeline of premium live content throughout the partnership

Beyond content, the partnership positioned Rooter as a serious player within India's esports ecosystem and became the blueprint for future collaborations, with multiple leading esports organizations joining the platform following OR Esports' lead.

The Outcome

By securing exclusive content and creator partnerships, Rooter moved from a technology platform competing on features to an audience platform competing on content. The OR Esports partnership was the credibility anchor that signaled to the market: "This is where esports happens."

The 50M+ views over 24 months proved the model worked. More importantly, Rooter established itself as an alternative destination: not just for viewers, but for creators and esports organizations looking for exclusive streaming partnerships. The blueprint created with OR Esports became the template for future platform collaborations, setting Rooter apart in a crowded category.

Lesson: In streaming, exclusivity is currency. Build around exclusive content, and the audience follows.
