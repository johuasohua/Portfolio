The challenge

Rooter was entering one of India's most competitive categories against YouTube Gaming and Loco. It had the technology but lacked the exclusive content and creator ecosystem to become a credible home for live esports.

Competing on features alone wasn't viable. Rooter needed a content anchor that told creators and audiences it was serious about esports.

The idea

I brokered a 24-month exclusive partnership that made OR Esports Rooter's flagship content partner, built on audience migration, not acquisition.

Creators started streams on YouTube, then moved their audiences to continue watching exclusively on Rooter. Rooter also secured exclusive digital rights to Legends Rise, OR Esports' flagship BGMI tournament, so it had premium content from day one.

The execution

16 pro athletes and creators streamed exclusively on Rooter for 24 months:

  • Competitive scrims and ranked gameplay from pro players
  • Creator-led entertainment and community streams
  • Two annual editions of Legends Rise, streamed exclusively
  • A steady pipeline of premium live content

The outcome

Rooter moved from a technology platform competing on features to an audience platform competing on content. The OR Esports deal was the credibility anchor that told the market where esports lived.

The blueprint became the template for Rooter's later platform partnerships, with other leading esports organizations following OR Esports onto the platform.
