The challenge

Intel was known for performance but losing mindshare in India's gaming community. AMD's lower price point made it the default for budget-conscious gamers building their first competitive rig.

The problem wasn't technology. It was perception: Intel read as premium-priced, AMD as value. Intel had to move the conversation from price to performance, in the place gamers actually decide: esports.

The idea

Put Intel where performance matters most. A 12-month partnership with OR Esports used elite players to prove a simple truth: competitive gaming is won in milliseconds, and hardware makes a measurable difference.

The shift: from "Intel is expensive" to "Intel is what pros use".

The execution

Intel became an integrated partner across the OR Esports ecosystem:

  • Branding on team jerseys and at tournament appearances
  • Year-round creator content with pro players on Intel-powered rigs
  • Live-stream mentions during competitive play
  • Instagram campaigns featuring partner laptops (Alienware, Acer)
  • Retail promotion linking straight to purchase points

The outcome

Authentic pro advocacy, always-on content and retail messaging shifted perception among competitive gamers. Intel wasn't just sponsoring esports. It showed why performance matters where gamers care most: winning.

The 18% sales increase for Intel-powered gaming laptops proved esports could drive conversion, not just awareness.
