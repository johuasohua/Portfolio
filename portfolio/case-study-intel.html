Intel × OR Esports

Intel Inside the Rock

How Intel re-established itself as the processor of choice for competitive gamers and drove measurable sales growth for partner retailers.

The Challenge

Intel processors had long been recognized for performance, but they were losing mindshare among India's gaming community. AMD's lower price point made it the preferred choice for budget-conscious gamers looking to build their first competitive rig.

For Intel, the problem wasn't technology; it was perception. Gamers saw Intel as premium-priced, while AMD was value-for-money. Intel needed to shift the conversation from price to performance where gamers actually made decisions: in esports.

The Idea

We positioned Intel where performance mattered most: professional esports. By partnering with OR Esports for a 12-month campaign, we leveraged the credibility of elite players to demonstrate a simple truth: competitive gaming is won on milliseconds, and high-performance hardware makes a measurable difference.

The strategic shift: Move from "Intel is expensive" to "Intel is what pros use." In esports, that's everything.

This wasn't about sponsorship logos. It was about authentic advocacy. Professional players, under real competitive pressure, choosing Intel-powered machines.

The Execution

Intel became an integrated partner across the OR Esports ecosystem:

  • Branding featured on team jerseys and tournament appearances
  • Creator content throughout the year showing pro players discussing Intel-powered gaming rigs
  • Live stream mentions during competitive play, highlighting performance advantages
  • Instagram campaigns showcasing partner gaming laptops (Alienware, Acer) with Intel processors
  • Direct retail promotion linking to purchase points

The campaign avoided the trap of "brand mentions." Instead, professional players under competitive pressure shared their genuine experiences using Intel-powered gaming machines, highlighting the performance, responsiveness, and reliability required to compete at the highest level.

The Outcome

By combining authentic esports advocacy with always-on content and retail messaging, Intel successfully shifted perception among competitive gamers. The brand wasn't just sponsoring esports; it was demonstrating why performance mattered in the one context where gamers cared most: winning.

The 18% sales increase for Intel-powered gaming laptops proved the strategy worked. Esports didn't just build brand awareness. It drove conversion. Gamers who watched pro players on Intel rigs decided they wanted the same edge.

Lesson: In gaming, authenticity beats messaging. Show, don't tell. Let the pros do the talking.
