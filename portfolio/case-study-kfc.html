The challenge

KFC wanted to connect with India's fast-growing gaming community but had never activated inside esports. The brand saw gamers as a high-value segment but lacked the cultural credibility to reach them authentically.

The category leader had already claimed mainstream esports. KFC needed an entry point that felt native to gaming culture, not borrowed from a sponsorship playbook.

The idea

KFC became title sponsor of Legends Rise and the tournament's official "Winner Winner Chicken Dinner" partner, a phrase every battle royale player already says when they win.

We created the KFC Loot Box: you earn loot in BGMI, you earn rewards through KFC. In-game reward mechanics met real-world prizes and exclusive offers.

The execution

The campaign ran across broadcast, app, creators and retail:

  • A custom 3D Colonel Sanders dropped into the live Legends Rise broadcast
  • Viewers screenshot the Colonel and share with KFC India to win merch and in-app discounts
  • The Loot Box meal launched exclusively on the KFC app
  • Creator-led content with one of India's largest gaming influencers
  • OR Esports branding across the team and tournament ecosystem

The outcome

By speaking the language and mechanics of esports, KFC earned cultural relevance with an audience that usually tunes out advertising.

The best gaming partnerships aren't logos on jerseys. They find the cultural insight that makes a brand belong in the moment. For BGMI players, "Winner Winner Chicken Dinner" was already a meme. We gave it a real world.
