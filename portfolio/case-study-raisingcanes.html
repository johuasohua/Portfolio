Raising Cane's × POWR

Raising POWR In KSA

How Raising Cane's built an always-on gaming layer with POWR in Saudi Arabia and turned gaming culture into product: 25M+ views and 14% of KSA transactions, just five months into a twelve-month partnership.

The Challenge

Raising Cane's had been in Saudi Arabia for ten years with 26 stores, yet aided awareness of the brand remained very low. Among its core Gen Z audience, the same people spending hours a day inside gaming and esports, Raising Cane's barely registered.

The insight was hard to ignore: 59% of Raising Cane's audience already watch gaming content on social. Gaming wasn't a subculture the brand had to break into, it was where the audience already spent its attention. Campaign bursts wouldn't move the needle. The brand needed permanent presence, not temporary visibility.

The Idea

Instead of relying on one-off brand activations, we built an always-on gaming layer: a permanent presence that sat alongside every Raising Cane's campaign through the year, structured across three layers spanning app installs, the World Cup moment, and trial generation.

We partnered the brand with POWR, the largest gaming creator cohort in KSA: 16 creator channels with 147M+ combined reach across the platforms where Saudi gaming actually lives, from YouTube and Kick to TikTok, Snapchat, Instagram and Discord.

The principle was simple. We didn't buy gaming media, we integrated Raising Cane's into gaming culture, so the brand showed up as a peer in the community rather than an ad interrupting it.

The Execution

The idea came to life through six connected mechanics, built inside the stream and finished in the store:

  • Online jersey design competition
  • In-store watch parties for live matches
  • TikTok challenge videos with creators
  • In-store creator meet-and-greets
  • Snapchat taste-test campaigns
  • Scan-and-win competitions powered by creator codes

Orders were delivered by creators on live streams, promo codes moved fans from feed to store, and branded meet-ups turned viewers into customers. One continuous conversation ran the whole way through: stream to social to store to creator to community to purchase.

It Reached Exactly Who It Was Built For

The collaboration over-indexed among Saudi Gen Z males aged 16 to 25, the exact audience the brand needed to win. In that group, gaming content isn't niche, it's habitual: 62% watch content from gamer creators and 7 in 10 watch it at least once a week.

The scale in context: 3.27M+ views and 273K watch hours on YouTube and Kick alone add up to more than 31 years of continuous viewing time, delivered in just five months.

The Strategic Shift

This partnership transformed gaming from a one-time activation channel into an always-on marketing layer for Raising Cane's. Instead of competing with category leaders on store count or tradition, the brand built permanent cultural relevance with the audience that mattered most.

"Gaming isn't a campaign channel. It's a lifestyle channel. The brands winning aren't the ones with the loudest one-week burst; they're the ones present every day."

By embedding Raising Cane's into POWR's ecosystem, the brand wasn't fighting for attention. It was part of the infrastructure. Gamers didn't see Raising Cane's as a sponsor interrupting their entertainment. They saw it as a peer in their community.
