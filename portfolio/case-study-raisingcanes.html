Raising Cane's × POWR

Always-On Gaming Partnership

How a challenger QSR brand became a permanent fixture in Saudi Arabia's gaming ecosystem—driving 22% brand recall growth in three months.

The Challenge

Raising Cane's was accelerating its expansion into Saudi Arabia but faced an uphill battle against category dominance. KFC operated over 100 stores; Raising Cane's had just 20. More critically, the brand lacked awareness and recall among its core 18–34-year-old demographic—the same audience spending hours in gaming and esports communities.

Traditional campaign bursts wouldn't move the needle. The brand needed a different strategy: permanent presence, not temporary visibility.

The Idea

Instead of relying on one-off brand activations, we built an always-on gaming strategy designed to keep Raising Cane's consistently relevant with Gen Z and Millennial consumers.

We partnered the brand with POWR, one of the Middle East's largest esports organizations, leveraging its highly engaged audience and digital ecosystem of over 150 million followers and subscribers across social platforms.

The partnership was designed to become a permanent gaming layer that amplified every Raising Cane's marketing campaign throughout the year, ensuring the brand was present wherever gaming audiences spent their time.

The Execution

We secured a 12-month strategic partnership with POWR, integrating Raising Cane's across multiple touchpoints:

  • Creator content featuring popular streamers and esports athletes
  • Integrated esports activations across POWR tournaments
  • Social media campaigns amplifying gaming moments
  • Branded entertainment and community engagement initiatives

Rather than existing as a standalone sponsorship logo, the partnership complemented and extended the reach of every major Raising Cane's campaign. The brand became woven into the gaming community's identity throughout the year.

The Strategic Shift

This partnership transformed gaming from a one-time activation channel into an always-on marketing layer for Raising Cane's. Instead of competing with category leaders on store count or tradition, the brand built permanent cultural relevance with the audience that mattered most.

"Gaming isn't a campaign channel. It's a lifestyle channel. The brands winning aren't the ones with the loudest one-week burst—they're the ones present every day."

By embedding Raising Cane's into POWR's ecosystem, the brand wasn't fighting for attention. It was part of the infrastructure. Gamers didn't see Raising Cane's as a sponsor interrupting their entertainment. They saw it as a peer in their community.
