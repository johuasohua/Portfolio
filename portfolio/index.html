The Live Hunt

Insight

Wingstop × PUBG Mobile

Wingstop: Road to Riyadh

Insight

Eating wings and playing mobile games occupy the same one-handed, screen-in-front moment. Wingstop wasn’t competing with gaming for attention; it lives inside it.

Idea

A three-tier strategy: an always-on platform layer, a hero tournament activation, and owned IP anchored by a “Buy Wingstop, win PUBG Mobile World Cup Finals tickets” mechanic across in-game, social and packaging.

Strategic bet

Treat the KSA gaming calendar as media: build around the Esports World Cup, the region’s record-breaking tentpole (750M viewers).

Alfa Romeo × PUBG Mobile

The Lap

Insight

The thrill of the perfect lap and the rush of the battle royale share the same adrenaline. Speed is the universal language, and Alfa Romeo's audience is already playing.

Idea

Alfa Romeo hosts a social media contest with PUBG Mobile, rewarding 8 players a chance to win a real "LAP" around Abu Dhabi's F1 circuit: a shotgun seat next to a pro driver in an Alfa Romeo. In return, PUBG Mobile features the Alfa Romeo car prominently in-game for millions of players to use.

The mechanic

Players submit their best in-game screenshot. 8 winners selected on creativity and flair. Reactions filmed at the circuit and shared across both brands' channels.
